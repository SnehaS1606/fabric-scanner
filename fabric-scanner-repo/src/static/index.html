1. Scan the fabric

Camera is off. Press "Start camera".

Hold the fabric as close as the camera stays sharp, so the weave fills the dashed frame (the zoom setting crops to that frame). When the picture is steady, the app takes one photo with the room light and one with the flash. If it never settles, it captures anyway after 8 seconds, or press Capture now.

Or scan a dataset sample (reliable demo)

3. Ask about this fabric

Scan a fabric, then ask a question by voice or text.

Voice: speak after the beep. Say "done" to stop. Press M again to stop listening.

Keyboard: S scan, M ask by voice, R repeat last answer. Answers are spoken aloud. Live-capture accuracy has not been validated; the classifier was trained on close-up dataset images.
